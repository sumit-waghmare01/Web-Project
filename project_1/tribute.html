Subhash Chandra Bose

give me a blood, i will give you freedom

Subhash Chandra Bose was a famous Indian freedom fighter who worked tirelessly to free India from British rule. He believed that India should become an independent nation and encouraged people to unite for freedom. He was born on 23 January 1897 in Cuttack, Odisha, India. Subhash Chandra Bose was known for his strong leadership and fearless personality. Because of his contributions to India’s independence movement, he became one of the most respected leaders in Indian history.
